Overview

The report and the presentation together make up 30% of the final grade. Both are based on one empirical research paper you choose.

ItemWeightDate
Presentation10%Nov 3 – Dec 15, Tuesdays and Thursdays (no presentations on Dec 3)
Report20%Due Friday, Dec 18, 2026, 11:59 PM

Paper (20%)

Choose a cognitive science topic, either one covered in class or one you are interested in. Select a related research paper and write about it, including an introduction to the study and your own suggestions.

  • Paper type: empirical (experimental) research articles only. Review articles are not accepted.
  • Length: about 5–10 pages
  • Format: free, but the report must cover the five parts below
  1. Purpose and background of the study
  2. Methods
  3. Results
  4. Limitations
  5. Suggestions

Deadline: Friday, December 18, 2026, 11:59 PM.

Example topics

Any topic related to human behavior and thought processes is welcome. Examples:

  • How does our brain perceive motion created by visual illusions?
  • How are stimuli we are not aware of (subliminal stimuli) processed in the brain?
  • How are unattended stimuli processed in the brain?
  • What happens in the brain while we dream?
  • Do bilingual speakers show different patterns of brain activation from monolingual speakers?
  • Do brain-training games work?

Presentation (10%)

Present your report in up to 10 minutes, including Q&A. One or two teams present at the start of each class. The format is free.

  • Dates: Tuesdays and Thursdays from November 3 to December 15. No presentations on December 3.
  • Presenters: yourself alone, or yourself and one other student

Choosing your date: email the instructor in advance with:

  1. The presenter(s): your name, and your partner's name if you present as a pair
  2. Your preferred date(s), in order of preference. If you can present on any date except a few, list the dates you cannot make instead.

Students who do not send an email will be assigned a date by the instructor.
